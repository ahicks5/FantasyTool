# Creators: commission first

**The deal:** their own link and code. They keep **40% of every first payment** (week or season).
Nothing up front. This week only, a **$25 to $50 bonus paid after they post**, for the first 3.
Paid by you through PayPal or Venmo. We track it in `/admin` → Channels, by `utm_medium=NAME`.

## Who to find (30 this week: 15 Tue, 15 Wed)

- **Size:** 5k to 50k followers. Big creators ignore commission; small ones say yes.
- **Where:** TikTok and YouTube Shorts ("start sit week 4", "waiver wire week 4"), X (fantasy
  analysts who post their own lineups), Instagram fantasy meme pages.
- **Signal they fit:** they answer follower questions, show their own team, post weekly.
- **Skip:** anyone pushing sportsbook or DFS promo codes. Wrong brand, and the audience wants bets, not calls.

Keep a list in a note: name, platform, followers, date pitched, reply, link sent, posted, sales.

## The pitch (DM or email; personalize the first line every time)

> Hey [name], [one specific line about a recent post of theirs].
>
> I built Penthouse, a fantasy football call sheet: link your league, get your start/sit, waiver
> bids and trade offers, each with a confidence stamp. Here's mine: [screenshot].
>
> Want your own link? You keep 40% of every first payment, forever. Plus $50 if you post about it
> this weekend. No script, just show your real team and whether it helped.
>
> I'll set you up with a free season so you can try it first.

## When they say yes

1. Comp their season: `/admin` → Accounts → find them after they sign up → Grant → Season.
2. Send their link: `https://penthousefantasy.com/?utm_source=creator&utm_medium=NAME&utm_campaign=wk4`
3. Send 3 things they can use: their own call sheet, the Lock card they can share, a 9-second demo.
4. Ask them to say **#ad** or "partner" in the post. That's the FTC rule, and it keeps them safe too.

## Follow-up (Thursday, to anyone who hasn't answered)

> No worries if not your thing. The offer stands all season. Lineups lock Sunday at 1 if you want
> to try it on your team first.

## Paying out

Every Monday: `/admin` → Channels → `creator` row → expand for each NAME. Their share is 40% of
the first payments shown. Pay within the week. Being the brand that pays fast is how you get the next 10.
