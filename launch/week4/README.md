## Rules that never bend

- **No accuracy numbers in public.** No "right 75% of the time", no hit rate. Show single calls,
  misses included. (CLAUDE.md; the old `launch/posts.md` predates this rule — do not reuse its numbers.)
- **Penthouse, one word.** Never "Penthouse Fantasy". Descriptor: *fantasy football call sheet*.
- **No betting or DFS words** anywhere near an ad.
- **Say "I built this"** on Reddit and in DMs. Always.
- **Spend rules:** an ad under 0.5% click-through after 1,000 views goes off; a channel with $50
  spent and no sign-up is off for the week; under $150 kept by Saturday noon, Sunday paid drops to $100.
